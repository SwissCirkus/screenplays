Screenplays — Privacy

SwissCirkus Screenplays, the script writer at swisscirkus.github.io/screenplays (Google sign-in shown as “SwissCirkus”). Last updated 16 November 2026.

The short version

Your scripts are yours. They are kept in your own browser and, if you sign in with Google, in your own Google Drive. There is no Screenplays server: we don't receive, store, read or sell your scripts, your keys or anything about you. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • In your browser (on your own computer): your scripts, projects, settings, and any AI keys you paste into the Script Assistant. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes them from that browser.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you sign in with Google: a copy of your projects and scripts, so they're on every device you use. Screenplays can only see the files it made itself there (Google's “drive.file” access), not the rest of your Drive.

Google sign-in and Google Docs

  • Sign in with Google is used only to keep your scripts in your own Drive. Your Google account details stay with Google; Screenplays only uses the sign-in to read and write its own files.
  • Google Docs access is optional and off until you turn it on in the Script Assistant. It lets the assistant find, read and (when you press Apply) write the Google Docs you ask it about, so it needs wider Drive access. You can turn it off there at any time, and remove it in your Google account under Security › Third-party access.
  • Screenplays' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Nothing from your Google account is used for anything except the features you use, and nothing is passed to anyone else.

When something leaves your computer

Only when you use a feature that needs it, and straight from your browser to the service — never through us:

  • Script Assistant: what you ask, with the parts of your script or Doc it needs, goes to the AI service whose key you entered (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), under your own account with them.
  • Voice: the microphone in Chrome, Edge or Safari uses the browser's own speech recognition (Google or Microsoft); otherwise your recording goes to OpenAI or Google with your key. Table Read voices go to the voice service you chose.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. Links you open (a video, a Doc) go to that site.

Inside Kontroll

When the writer runs inside the Kontroll desktop app, Kontroll may answer the Script Assistant with the AI services set up in Kontroll on your own computer. That stays between your computer and those services.

Questions or deleting your data

Everything is in your browser and your own Drive, so you can delete it yourself at any time: clear the site's data in your browser, and delete the “Screenplays” folder in your Google Drive. For anything else, write to the support address shown on Google's “Sign in with Google” screen for this app.
